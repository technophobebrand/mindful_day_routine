About Mindful Day Routine

This web application helps you structure your daily routine with mindfulness, quiet presence, and awareness inspired by the traditions of Plum Village (founded by Zen Master Thich Nhat Hanh).

The Bell of Mindfulness

In Plum Village practice, the bell is a sacred friend. Whenever you hear the bell—whether during activity transitions or periodic reminders—stop whatever you are doing, cease talking, relax your body, and take 3 conscious, peaceful breaths.

Background Permissions & Notifications

To ensure notifications and bells work smoothly when running in the background:

  • Click the Notifications bell icon in the top header bar to grant browser permissions.
  • When a push notification fires, a bell sound will play along with a clear notification message detailing exactly why the bell rang.
